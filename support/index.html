Support

A little help, at hand.

Amano is made by M&D in Madrid. Email us directly at dani@getamano.app for help with the app, purchase access or privacy questions — Daniel reads and answers every email himself.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Please do not attach identity documents, passwords or payment details.

Sync between devices

Sync Amano between your iPhone and iPad: set up Unlimited and iCloud, check your documents arrived, and resolve common sync problems.

Trial and purchase

New customers can try every feature for seven days. After the trial, one payment unlocks full access permanently; there is no subscription or automatic charge. The App Store shows the local price before payment.

Already purchased?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. An internet connection is needed to check the purchase. This restores Unlimited access, not your stored documents.

Looking for your documents?

Check Archived and Recently Deleted. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting. With Amano Unlimited, your encrypted vault can sync through your own iCloud. On a new iPhone or iPad, restore purchase access if needed, check iCloud and Keychain on both devices, and use Sync now. Restoring a purchase alone does not restore documents. Keep independent copies of important originals.

Watermarks and signatures

When sharing a document, tap Share and choose Watermark to type the recipient and purpose, or Signature to place your saved signature; the preview shows the exact copy before it leaves your iPhone, and the original remains unchanged. During the 7-day trial, every feature is available without document or folder limits. After the trial, saved documents remain viewable and exportable as plain copies; the one-time Unlimited purchase restores adding documents and marked copies. Save or replace your signature in Settings.

Amano 1.3.5 removed the contact card, QR code and Event mode. Any saved card is deleted from the device the first time the app opens after the update.
