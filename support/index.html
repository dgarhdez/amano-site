Support

A little help, at hand.

Amano is developed by Daniel Garcia. Email dgarhdez@gmail.com for help with the app, purchase access or privacy questions.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Please do not attach identity documents, passwords or payment details.

Already purchased?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. An internet connection is needed to check the purchase. This restores Unlimited access, not your stored documents.

Looking for your documents?

Check Archived and Recently Deleted. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting. The public release stores documents locally and does not offer automatic document sync. Keep independent copies of important originals.

Watermarks and contact cards

Set your default watermark text and density in Settings. When sharing a PDF or image, choose a watermarked copy to customize the text and preview the result. This is an Unlimited feature; the original remains unchanged.

Create and edit your free card in the Contact card tab. A QR code shares contact text; the contact file can also include your photo. Show on Lock Screen starts Event mode. Use Stop Event mode in the same tab to end it.

Refunds

For App Store charges, follow Apple’s refund instructions.
