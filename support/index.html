Support

A little help, at hand.

Amano is developed by Daniel Garcia. Email dgarhdez@gmail.com for help with the app, purchase access or privacy questions.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Please do not attach identity documents, passwords or payment details.

Already purchased?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. An internet connection is needed to check the purchase. This restores Unlimited access, not your stored documents.

Looking for your documents?

Check Archived and Recently Deleted. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting. If you use manual iCloud snapshots, keep independent originals: replacement-device recovery has not yet been verified in this first version.

Refunds

For App Store charges, follow Apple’s refund instructions.
