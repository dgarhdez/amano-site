Free and Unlimited in version 1.3.2

Starting with Amano 1.3.2, a new or empty vault receives a free allowance of five document records and two private folders. If your vault already contains document records when first opened in version 1.3.2, it receives a ten-document free allowance instead. This allowance is saved for that vault; the free folder allowance remains two. These limits do not restrict an active Unlimited purchase.

A document with multiple pages or attachments counts as one record. Archived documents count toward the document limit; Recently Deleted documents do not count toward the active limit. Free access includes local storage, scanning and import, PDF merging, signatures, expiry dates, your contact card and QR code, Face ID protection and widgets. Advance expiry reminders require Unlimited.

Amano Unlimited is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited document records and private folders, subfolders and color labels, iCloud sync and backup, shared family folders, advance expiry reminders, reusable document packs, watermarked PDF and image copies, and Lock Screen Event mode. There is no subscription or recurring fee for this purchase. “Unlimited” removes the app’s document and folder count limits; storage remains subject to the capacity of your device and, when used, your iCloud account.

The purchase covers the Unlimited features described at checkout. It does not include additional iCloud storage or a guarantee of indefinite third-party cloud-service availability.

Contact cards and copies

Creating and sharing your contact card is free. Lock Screen Event mode requires Unlimited. Only include information you intend to share. Starting Event mode makes the selected contact details and QR code visible on the Lock Screen until you stop it or the activity ends. Shared contact files and copies saved by recipients cannot be recalled.

Watermarks indicate the intended use of exported copies. They are not access controls and cannot prevent copying, editing or removal. Watermarked copies are generated separately; your originals remain unchanged.

Your documents and responsibilities

You retain rights to your documents. Only store or share material you are entitled to use, including documents belonging to another person. Exporting a document sends a copy to your chosen recipient or service; Amano cannot recall those copies.

Amano is an organizational tool, not an official identity credential, certified archive or source of legal advice. A scan may not replace an original document. Check extracted text and dates yourself, and do not rely solely on expiry reminders for important deadlines.

Storage and backups

The private vault is stored encrypted on your device. Unlimited includes optional iCloud sync and backup for your private vault and shared family folders through Apple’s CloudKit. These features depend on your Apple Account, available iCloud storage, connectivity and Apple’s services. People you invite can access documents in the family folders you share. Keep independent copies of important originals; a purchase restore is not a document backup or recovery operation.

Recently Deleted remains recoverable and does not automatically erase documents. Deleting local records does not remove separate source files, exported copies or old iCloud backups. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
